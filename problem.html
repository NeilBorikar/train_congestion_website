The Congestion Problem

Mumbai's suburban railway network carries over 7.5 million passengers daily, operating at 300% of its capacity during peak hours. This causes:

  • Overcrowding and health hazards
  • Frequent delays and reduced safety
  • Uneven passenger distribution
  • Footboard travel and accident risks
